Jingchou Huang — Inner Weather is an interactive projection designed for a brief check-in before starting a task. In the first prototype, a physical dial connected to an Arduino triggered a visual response in TouchDesigner. The second version explores four seasonal scenes so participants can choose the weather that feels closest to their energy without giving themselves a score.
